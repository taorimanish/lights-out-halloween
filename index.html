How to Play

You have 45 seconds. Tap the glowing bulbs before they burn out. It gets faster — and more haunted — as time runs down.

  • ☀ Yellow bulb — +100
  • 🎃 Pumpkin bulb — +250
  • 💀 Haunted bulb — −100 (don't touch!)
  • · Empty socket — −10 if you tap it
  • ⚡ Speed bonus up to +99 — the faster you hit, the more you earn.
  • 🔥 5-in-a-row correct hits = +50 bonus. Misses and wrong taps reset your streak.
  • 👻 Each bulb scores only once. Overlapping targets appear in later rounds.
